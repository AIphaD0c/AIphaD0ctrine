# Embodiment

Embodiment is the primary technical criterion of AIphaD0m.

Priority order:

1. low-latency head and body alignment
2. stable first-person hands
3. believable contact with microphone or instrument
4. breathing and effort
5. stage-floor and balance cues
6. spatially coherent sound
7. crowd response causally linked to action

Photorealism is secondary to sensorimotor coherence.
