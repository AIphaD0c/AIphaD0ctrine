# Vision

## Objective

AIphaD0m seeks to make a mediated performance feel autobiographical.

The participant should experience a stadium-scale AIphaD0c performance from the first-person position of the performer. The system should progressively reduce the psychological distance between participant, body, environment, memory, and signal.

## What AIphaD0m is

AIphaD0m is:

- a perceptual domain
- an embodiment system
- a dramaturgical engine
- a memory-construction experiment
- a controlled identity-transfer experience
- a future interface between generated media and subjective presence

## What AIphaD0m is not

AIphaD0m is not:

- a standard music video
- a passive 360-degree concert
- a conventional rhythm game
- an avatar customization platform
- a digital twin of a real person
- a claim that generated events truly occurred

## Experience thesis

A believable first-person concert memory requires more than photorealism.

The system must coordinate:

1. body ownership
2. gaze and head motion
3. hand and instrument continuity
4. spatial sound
5. crowd causality
6. tension and release
7. memory-like compression and omission
8. controlled inconsistencies that reveal the artificial domain

## Narrative arc

The preferred long-form arc is:

1. **Arrival** — the participant is present but identity remains stable.
2. **Alignment** — movement, sound, hands, and stage feedback synchronize.
3. **Ownership** — the performance begins to feel self-generated.
4. **Expansion** — the stadium reacts as an extension of the participant.
5. **Corruption** — details contradict ordinary reality.
6. **Identity fracture** — it becomes unclear whether the participant became AIphaD0c or AIphaD0c became the participant.
7. **Residual memory** — the event ends, but the memory remains subjectively owned.

## Long-term statement

Anyone may enter the domain.

Not everyone must leave with the same identity.
